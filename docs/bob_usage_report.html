3. Detailed Session 1 — Plan Mode: Energy Accounting & Cut Disjointness Gate G-13 Trace

Session 1: Architectural Plan & Defect Preemption

Plan Mode • Hours 0–2
Exact Engineer Prompt
"Review the energy balance reconciliation formula across feeders, transformers, and branches. Identify any risks of double-counting downstream energy or unit inconsistency between power (kW) and energy (kWh). Critique how missing meter values should be handled."
Starting Defect / Fragility: Initial prototype sketches aggregated sections hierarchically without disjoint cuts, risking that transformer boundaries would sum both feeder branches and child household meters. Missing readings were also proposed to be zero-filled or median-imputed.
Bob's Architectural Guidance:
  • Double-Counting Hazard: Mandated that section boundaries must evaluate only immediate instrumented cuts. Nested descendants are forbidden.
  • Quadratic Loss Unit Inconsistency: Flagged that $b \cdot P^2 \cdot \Delta t$ requires power in $\text{kW}$. 15-minute readings in $\text{kWh}$ must be normalized: $P = \text{kWh} / 0.25\text{ h}$.
  • Imputation Hazard: Rejected zero-filling missing values; missing values must be preserved as `None` to prevent false positive theft alarms.
Human Review Decision: ACCEPTED: Immediate cut validation in backend/topology.py; power unit normalization; preserving None.
REJECTED: Bob recommended an automated AC power-flow iterative solver. We rejected this as unnecessary overhead for the 24-hour prototype, keeping the reduced-order $(a, b)$ physical model.
